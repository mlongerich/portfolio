### **Additional content:**
- LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/mlongerich
- GitHub: https://github.com/mlongerich (Ruby projects visible)
- Text block:

For each turn of the game, my bot generated every legal move, evaluated each option against a set of weighted heuristics, and selected the highest-scoring action.

I continually refined the weighting system by testing against previous versions of the bot, analyzing losses, and adjusting the evaluation criteria. The final solution was built entirely with custom logic and iterative experimentation rather than machine learning or third-party AI libraries.
